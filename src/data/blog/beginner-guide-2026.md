# Ultimate Devil Hunter Roblox Beginner Guide (2026)

Welcome to the world of Devil Hunter! This guide is designed to help new players navigate the early game, choose the right contracts, and level up efficiently.

## Best Starter Contract

Choosing your first contract is crucial. While you might be tempted to roll for a Legendary immediately, some Common or Rare contracts are actually better for leveling.

### Recommendation: Smoke or Bomb

*   **Smoke Devil:** excellent for maneuverability and dodging early game bosses.
*   **Bomb Devil:** Great AoE (Area of Effect) damage, making it easy to clear groups of weak mobs.

Avoid "Mantis" or "Ghost" early on unless you have high skill, as they require precise combos.

## How to Level Up Fast

The fastest way to level up in Devil Hunter is by completing the **Quest Loop**.

1.  **Accept Quests:** Always pick up quests from NPCs near your level.
2.  **Group Up:** Join a party if possible. You get shared XP for kills.
3.  **Daily Raids:** Once you hit level 20, participate in the Zombie Raid for massive XP drops.

**Pro Tip:** Don't just grind random mobs. The XP per hour is much lower than completing structured quests.

## What to Spend Yen On First?

Yen is the main currency, and you shouldn't waste it.

1.  **Surgery Kit (3,500 Yen):** This is your #1 priority. It allows you to harvest hearts and limbs to get better contracts later.
2.  **Health Potions:** Always carry at least 10.
3.  **Basic Katana:** If you aren't using a transformation contract, a sword serves as a good backup weapon.

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

*   **Rerolling too early:** Don't waste your free rerolls until you understand which playstyle you like.
*   **Ignoring Stats:** Focus on one stat (Strength or Devil Fruit) rather than spreading them out evenly.
*   **Attacking High-Level Players:** PvP is enabled in most areas. Don't pick fights you can't win!
