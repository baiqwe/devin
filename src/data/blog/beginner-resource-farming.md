# How to Get Stone & Resources in The Forge (Complete Guide)

**Stuck trying to find Stone in The Forge?** You're not alone - this is one of the most searched questions for new players. This guide covers the fastest ways to farm Stone, along with a complete ore depth chart for all resources.

---

## How to Get Stone (Fast Method)

Stone is the most basic resource in The Forge, but new players often struggle to find it efficiently.

### Stone Location: Quick Answer

> **Stone spawns at Depth 0-100m in any mining area.** You don't need to go deep - stay near the surface!

### Step-by-Step Stone Farming

1. **Enter any Mine** (Stonewake Mine is closest to spawn)
2. **Stay in the first 50 meters** - don't go deeper!
3. **Look for gray rock formations** - these are Stone nodes
4. **Mine with any pickaxe** - even the starting Stone Pickaxe works
5. **Collect 20-30 Stone per minute** using this method

### Common Mistake: Going Too Deep

Many new players think deeper = better. **This is wrong for Stone.**

As you go deeper:
- Stone becomes **less common**
- It gets replaced by Iron, Copper, and rarer ores
- Your time is wasted searching instead of mining

> **Pro Tip:** If you need Stone for early crafting (like the Anvil or Furnace), stay at 0-30m depth for the fastest farming.

## Beginner Mining Route (First 30 Minutes)

Here's the optimal farming path for new players:

### Phase 1: Starter Gear (0-10 min)

1. Mine **20 Stone** at 0-30m depth
2. Mine **10 Coal** at 10-50m depth
3. Mine **15 Copper** at 25-50m depth
4. **Return to surface** and craft basic gear

### Phase 2: Iron Farming (10-20 min)

1. Descend to **50-100m depth**
2. Mine **20 Iron Ore**
3. Smelt into Iron Ingots
4. Craft [Iron Pickaxe](/item/iron-pickaxe) for faster mining

### Phase 3: Profit Run (20-30 min)

1. Descend to **100-150m depth**
2. Mine **Silver and Gold** as you see them
3. Sell at the surface merchant
4. Use Gold to buy upgrades

## Tips for Efficient Mining

### 1. Always Bring Coal

You need Coal to smelt ores into Ingots. Bring **at least 20 Coal** per mining trip to avoid wasted travel time.

### 2. Use the Right Pickaxe

| Pickaxe | Max Efficient Depth |
|---------|---------------------|
| Stone Pickaxe | 100m |
| Iron Pickaxe | 500m |
| Mythril Pickaxe | 800m |
| [Arcane Pickaxe](/item/arcane-pickaxe) | 1000m+ |
| [Prismatic Pickaxe](/item/prismatic-pickaxe) | 1200m+ |

### 3. Inventory Management

- **Don't hoard Stone** - it's not worth the inventory space at deep levels
- **Stack rare ores first** - they're harder to find again
- **Smelt on the go** if you find a Portable Furnace

## Resource FAQ

### "Why can't I find any Gold?"

Gold starts spawning at **150m depth**. If you're above that, you won't find any.

### "What's the best ore for making money?"

At early game: **Iron** (easy to find, consistent profit)
At late game: **Galaxite** from boss drops (highest value)

### "How do I mine faster?"

1. Upgrade your pickaxe (see chart above)
2. Get the **Dwarf race** (+25% mining speed)
3. Equip [Miner Shard runes](/item/rune-miner-shard) for bonus stats
