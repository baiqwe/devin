# The Forge (Roblox) Boss Guide: Combat Gimmicks & Rune Drops

**Why am I doing zero damage?**

Friends, I was struggling with combat in **The Forge on Roblox**. I'd hack-and-slash through normal enemies, but when I faced a proper Boss, I'd get demolished.

I quickly realized that bosses here aren't simple HP sponges. They are puzzle challenges with a core **"Gimmick."**

I had to study combat logs and advanced guides to survive. Here is how to deconstruct the challenges.

---

## The Stone Golem: The Armor Phase

My first brutal lesson. My mighty [Greatsword](/blog/weapon-mastery-katana-greatsword) was barely scratching it.

**The Gimmick:**
The Golem has a near-impenetrable "Armor Phase" where damage is reduced by 90%.

**The Solution:**
Wait for the **Seismic Slam**. When it raises its arm, it exposes a glowing, soft weak point on its back.

**My Strategy:**
1. Wait for the slam animation.
2. Dodge Roll behind it.
3. Unleash a focused combo on the weak spot.

I no longer attack wildly. I wait for the **Vulnerability Window**.

## The Flame Hound: Elemental Counters

This boss was burning me alive.

**The Gimmick:**
High fire resistance and constant burn aura.

**The Solution:**
I found an article explaining it is extremely vulnerable to **Frost Damage**.

I temporarily put aside my favorite fire-enchanted Katana and crafted a cheap **Ice-Runed Dagger**.
- **Result:** Massive damage multiplier.
- **Lesson:** The game rewards strategic preparation.

Check my [Enhancement Guide](/blog/enhancement-and-rune-experiments) for how to craft elemental runes.

## The Shadow Wraith: The Light Totem

The most challenging gimmick I've mastered so far.

**The Gimmick:**
The Wraith has an "Invulnerability Phase" where it becomes a swirling shadow. You cannot hit it.

**The Solution:**
You MUST place a **Light Totem** nearby (crafted by Engineers).
- The light forces the Wraith to materialize.
- Without the totem, you simply cannot win.

This requires preparation before you even enter the [Dungeon](/blog/dungeon-hidden-bosses-guide).

## The Goblin Lord: Rare Heart Boss

The **Goblin Lord** is the most sought-after boss right now because he drops the [Stolen Heart](/item/stolen-heart) - the rarest crafting material in the game.

**The Gimmick:**
Unlike other bosses, the Goblin Lord must be **summoned** through a tribute system at the Goblin Shrine in the Forgotten Kingdom.

**How to Spawn:**
1. Reach the Forgotten Kingdom via [Portal](/blog/portal-adventure-forgotten-kingdom)
2. Find the Goblin Shrine (central altar with goblin statues)
3. Offer three escalating tributes:
   - **Tribute 1:** 5 Gold Ore + 1 Topaz
   - **Tribute 2:** 10 Gold Ore + 3 Topaz
   - **Tribute 3:** 15 Gold Ore + 5 Topaz + 1 Ruby

**Combat Strategy:**
- **HP:** 50,000 (highest in the game)
- **Summons:** Goblin Minions spawn at 75%, 50%, and 25% HP
- **Critical Attack:** Ground Pound (2-second wind-up, massive AoE)

**My Strategy:**
1. **Gear up** with at least a [Demonite Axe](/item/demonite-axe)
2. **Priority Target:** Kill minions immediately when they spawn
3. **Dodge Window:** The Ground Pound animation is your signal to dodge
4. **DPS Phase:** Attack during the Taunt phase after Ground Pound

**Drop Rates:**
| Drop | Chance |
|------|--------|
| Ruby | 50% |
| Gold Gain Rune | 30% |
| Goblin Crown | 15% |
| **Stolen Heart** | **~2%** |

## The Raven: Aerial Predator

The **Raven** is a newer boss that many players struggle with due to its unique flying mechanics.

**The Gimmick:**
The Raven spends 70% of the fight **airborne** and unreachable by melee attacks. You must force it to land.

**Location:**
- **Zone:** Dark Forest (behind the Witch's Hut)
- **Spawn Time:** Dusk cycle only (every 10 in-game minutes)

**The Solution:**
The Raven is vulnerable to **Ranged Weapons**. I kept my [Tactical Crossbow](/blog/ranged-weapons-tactical-guide) specifically for this fight.

When you deal 20% of its HP with ranged attacks, it becomes **Grounded** for 15 seconds. This is your melee window.

**Combat Phases:**
1. **Flight Phase:** Dodge swooping attacks, fire ranged weapons
2. **Ground Phase:** Rush in with your highest DPS weapon
3. **Enrage (20% HP):** Attack speed doubles, stay mobile

**My Strategy:**
1. Equip a Crossbow or Throwing Daggers as secondary weapon
2. Dodge the **Swoop Attack** by rolling sideways (not backward)
3. When grounded, activate your best burst combo
4. Repeat until defeated

**Drop Table:**
| Drop | Chance |
|------|--------|
| Dark Feathers (Cosmetic) | 40% |
| Shadow Rune | 25% |
| Raven Mask (Helmet) | 10% |
| **Raven's Talon** (Legendary Dagger) | **~3%** |
