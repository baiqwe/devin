# How to Become "The Strongest Devil Hunter" (2026 Guide)

In the world of Devil Hunter, the title of **"The Strongest"** isn't just for show. It represents mastery over the game's mechanics, contracts, and raids. 

Recently, the search for "The Strongest Devil Hunter" has skyrocketed, mainly due to the new **Test of the Strongest** quest line. This guide will help you claim that title.

## The "Test of the Strongest" Quest

This is currently the hardest PVE challenge in the game.

*   **Location:** The Hidden Dojo (requires level 50+)
*   **Boss:** The Sensei (Mirror Match)
*   **Reward:** The "Flash Step" ability (crucial for PVP)

### Strategy to Win
1.  **Don't rely on m1 spam.** The Sensei will perfect block everything.
2.  **Use AoE.** Contracts like **Bomb** or **Dragon** work best here because the splash damage ignores some blocks.
3.  **Kiting is key.** Keep your distance and wait for cooldowns.

## The Meta Build (January 2026)

To truly be the strongest, you need the strongest gear.

### Best Contract: Eraser Devil
The [Eraser Devil](/wiki/contracts/eraser-devil) is currently the only S+ Tier contract for PVP. Its ability to delete enemy projectiles breaks the game balance.

### Best Fiend: Chainsaw Man
While rare (0.1% drop rate), the regeneration from the Chainsaw Heart makes you nearly unkillable in 1v1s.

### Best Clan: Royal Clan
The stamina buff allows you to dash twice as often as other players, which is essential for the high-speed combat of 2026 updates.

## Obtaining the Eraser Devil

Since you're here to be the strongest, you probably want the Eraser Devil. It cannot be rolled with standard rerolls. You must complete the **Void Quest** or use a **Legendary Ticket**.
