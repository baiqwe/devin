## What is the Surgery Kit?

The **Surgery Kit** is a crucial utility item in Devil Hunter Roblox. It's used to extract body parts from defeated fiends, devils, or other players. These harvested parts are essential for:

- **Crafting** powerful items and gear
- **Upgrading** your equipment
- **Forming contracts** with certain devils (like Leech Devil and Mold Devil)
- **Achieving transformations** such as becoming a hybrid or a fiend

## How to Get a Surgery Kit

You can obtain a Surgery Kit through several methods:

### 1. Medical Training Quest (Free)
Upon reaching **level 10**, you can complete the Medical Training quest chain, which rewards you with a **free Surgery Kit**. This is the recommended method for new players.

### 2. Purchase from Headquarters
You can buy a Surgery Kit from the Headquarters:
- Head to the **Division 2 Captain's room**
- Find it on a table near the **Crafting Bench**
- **Initial cost:** 500 Yen (during Division 6 Captain quest "Bound In Blood")
- **Regular cost:** 3,500 Yen

### 3. Black Market
The Surgery Kit can be purchased from the Black Market merchant for **5,000 to 10,000 Yen**.

### 4. Raid Drops
Occasionally, a Surgery Kit can drop as loot from successful **Raids**.

## Step-by-Step: How to Use the Surgery Kit

Follow these steps to successfully harvest body parts:

### Step 1: Defeat an Enemy
First, you must defeat a fiend, devil, or another player in combat. The enemy must be in a "downed" state.

### Step 2: Equip the Surgery Kit
Open your inventory and equip the Surgery Kit to your hotbar. Make sure it's easily accessible.

### Step 3: Approach and Initiate Extraction
- Get close to the defeated enemy
- Press the **M1** (left mouse button) or **interact key** to begin the extraction process

### Step 4: Wait for the Animation
A short animation will play while you extract the parts. **Stay still and remain uninterrupted** during this time - if you're attacked or move, the extraction will fail!

### Step 5: Collect Your Parts
If successful, the harvested body parts will be added directly to your inventory. Check your inventory to see what you obtained.

## Harvestable Body Parts

With the Surgery Kit, you can collect various body parts with different rarities:

| Rarity | Parts | Uses |
|--------|-------|------|
| **Common** | Devil Flesh | Fiend transformation, Fear accumulation |
| **Uncommon** | Limbs (Arms/Legs), Devil Eye | Mold Devil contract (requires legs) |
| **Rare** | Devil Heart | Leech Devil contract (requires hearts) |

## Tips for Successful Extraction

### Act Quickly
Extract **immediately** after defeating a fiend. The body will "decay" and eventually despawn, reducing your success chances the longer you wait.

### Check Your Inventory
Always have at least **one free inventory slot** before attempting an extraction. If your inventory is full, the extraction will fail!

### Unlimited Uses
Good news: The Surgery Kit has **unlimited uses**! Failed attempts only cost time and the specific mob, not the kit itself. Don't stress about wasting it.

### Be Aware of Your Surroundings
Harvesting in public areas can be dangerous. Other players might initiate **PvP** while you're vulnerable during the extraction animation. Find a safe spot or have teammates watch your back.

### Rarity Matters
Rarer enemies may have **lower success rates** for extraction. You might need to defeat multiple enemies to get specific parts. Don't give up!

## Contracts That Require Surgery Kit

Some contracts in Devil Hunter require specific body parts that can only be obtained with the Surgery Kit:

### Leech Devil Contract
- **Requires:** Human Hearts
- **How to get:** Use Surgery Kit on defeated players or NPCs

### Mold Devil Contract
- **Requires:** Human Legs
- **How to get:** Use Surgery Kit on defeated players or NPCs

## Common Problems and Solutions

### "Extraction Failed" Error
- **Cause:** Inventory full or you moved during animation
- **Solution:** Clear inventory space, stay still during extraction

### Can't Find Surgery Kit in Shop
- **Cause:** Haven't completed required quests
- **Solution:** Complete Division 6 Captain quest "Bound In Blood" first

### Low Success Rate
- **Cause:** Targeting high-rarity enemies
- **Solution:** Start with weaker enemies to practice, then move to rarer targets
