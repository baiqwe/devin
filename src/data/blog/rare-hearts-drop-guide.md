# The Forge Hearts Guide: Complete Drop Guide (2026)

**Everyone is asking: "How do I get the Stolen Heart?"**

Hearts are the most sought-after rare drops in The Forge Roblox right now. These powerful crafting materials are essential for end-game builds, but their drop rates are brutally low. This guide covers exactly how to obtain each heart, including spawn conditions, drop rates, and optimal farming strategies.

## Stolen Heart: Goblin Lord Drop

The **Stolen Heart** is the rarest and most valuable heart in The Forge. It drops exclusively from the **Goblin Lord** boss.

### How to Spawn the Goblin Lord

The Goblin Lord doesn't spawn naturally - you must trigger him through a specific sequence:

1. **Location:** Travel to the Forgotten Kingdom through the [Portal](/blog/portal-adventure-forgotten-kingdom)
2. **Tribute System:** You must offer escalating tributes to the Goblin Shrine
   - First Tribute: 5 Gold Ore + 1 Topaz
   - Second Tribute: 10 Gold Ore + 3 Topaz
   - Final Tribute: 15 Gold Ore + 5 Topaz + 1 Ruby
3. **Spawn Trigger:** After the third tribute, the Goblin Lord spawns at the shrine

### Goblin Lord Combat Strategy

The Goblin Lord is one of the toughest bosses in the game:

**Boss Stats:**
- **HP:** 50,000
- **Damage:** Very High (one-shot potential on low-armor builds)
- **Mechanic:** Summons Goblin Minions every 25% HP lost

**Recommended Strategy:**
1. Bring a [Demonite Axe](/item/demonite-axe) or better weapon
2. Clear minions immediately when they spawn
3. Dodge the **Ground Pound** attack (2-second wind-up animation)
4. Attack during his **Taunt Phase** after the ground pound

**Drop Table:**
- Gold Gain Rune: 30%
- Goblin Crown (Cosmetic): 15%
- **Stolen Heart: ~2%**
- Ruby: 50%

> **Pro Tip:** The Goblin Lord has a 30-minute respawn cooldown. If you fail, you'll need to wait or server-hop.

## Prismatic Heart: Winter Expansion Reward

The **Prismatic Heart** is obtained through the Winter Expansion content, not as a random drop.

### How to Get the Prismatic Heart

1. **Prerequisite:** Own the [Prismatic Pickaxe](/blog/prismatic-pickaxe-melting-geyser-guide)
2. **Location:** Frostspire Expanse - Crystal Caverns
3. **Quest Chain:**
   - Speak to the **Ice Sage NPC** near the Frozen Waterfall
   - Complete "The Frozen Heart" quest series (3 parts)
   - Mine 50 Iceite Ore for the final quest stage

### Prismatic Heart Uses

Unlike other hearts, the Prismatic Heart is used for:
- Crafting the **Prismatic Blade** (highest Light damage weapon)
- Upgrading the Prismatic Pickaxe to +1 tier
- Trading for 3x Race Rerolls at the Winter Merchant

## Yeti Heart: Frostspire Boss

The **Yeti Heart** drops from the Yeti boss in the Frostspire Expanse winter area.

### Yeti Location & Spawn

- **Zone:** Frostspire Expanse (Winter Expansion required)
- **Spawn Point:** Blizzard Peak (highest point on the map)
- **Spawn Timer:** Every 15 minutes (natural spawn)

### Yeti Combat Tips

The Yeti is an ice-elemental boss with freezing attacks:

**Key Mechanics:**
1. **Frost Breath:** Cone attack that freezes you for 3 seconds
2. **Avalanche:** AoE stomp - jump to avoid
3. **Enrage Phase:** Below 20% HP, attack speed doubles

**Counter Strategy:**
- Bring **Fire-enchanted weapons** for 2x damage multiplier
- [Flame Spark Rune](/item/rune-flame-spark) is extremely effective
- Keep moving to avoid freeze stacking

**Drop Table:**
- Snowite Ore: 40%
- Iceite Ore: 30%
- Frost Rune: 20%
- **Yeti Heart: ~3%**

## Golem Heart: Classic Boss Drop

The **Golem Heart** is the most accessible heart, dropping from both Stone Golem and Obsidian Golem.

### Stone Golem Location

- **Primary Zone:** Stonewake Mine (Depth 300-500m)
- **Spawn:** Natural spawn every 20 minutes

Refer to our [Stone Golem tactics](/blog/boss-combat-gimmicks-runes#the-stone-golem-the-armor-phase) for detailed combat strategy.

### Obsidian Golem (Higher Drop Rate)

The rare **Obsidian Golem** variant has double the heart drop rate:

- **Location:** Volcanic Depths (Depth 600m+)
- **Spawn:** Rare spawn (10% chance instead of Stone Golem)
- **Drop Rate:** Golem Heart at ~10%

### Golem Heart Uses

- Crafting **Golem Armor** (highest Defense set)
- Ingredient for the **Stone Shield** (blocks 1 hit completely)
- Required for "Golem Slayer" achievement
